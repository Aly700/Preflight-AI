RETROSPECTIVE — Ownership Became Ambiguous at Shift Change
Date: 2026-07-17
Program: DEMO-PROGRAM-BETA
Recorder: PERSON-0011, TEAM-0005

What happened
At a hydraulics desk change, the incoming coordinator read the recorder's name as the receiving owner. The outgoing coordinator had intended it only as the note's author. The resulting calls went to the writer while the team scope question remained outside the handover conversation. ISSUE-0112, TASK-0299, and TASK-0300 locate the review folder.

What helped
The outgoing desk had retained its dated carry list. Reading the team entry beside the next action showed what responsibility had actually been intended. The review discussion separated authorship, paper collection, and ownership instead of assigning all of them to whoever had written the clearest note.

What changes
Use separate owning-team and receiving-role spaces on the transfer sheet. Apply receiver echo: the receiver repeats the next coordination action and its due date, and the sending clerk records that response in the transfer margin. A signature-like initial or presence at a conversation is insufficient.

If the receiving role is uncertain, the outgoing desk keeps the next contact and asks the duty chair to resolve the scope before shift end. The uncertainty must travel with the action, not disappear into an unlabelled note.

Follow-up
PERSON-0011 will walk through the revised transfer margin with the roster clerk on 2026-07-20. Keep the original ambiguous note as a teaching copy beside the corrected layout.
