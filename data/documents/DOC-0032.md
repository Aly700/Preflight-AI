MEMORANDUM
Date: 2026-08-23
To: DEMO-PROGRAM-BETA queue coordinators
From: PERSON-0010, TEAM-0004
Subject: Priority Change Freeze During the BETA Records Audit

Decision: DEMO-PROGRAM-BETA will freeze changes to existing priority fields during the records audit week, 2026-08-24 through 2026-08-28. This is an administrative comparison period so the audit clerks can trace decisions against a stable set of dated papers.

New issues still receive an initial priority and written reason at intake. The freeze concerns changes to an existing rank; it leaves the intake requirement and the ownership and follow-up duties intact.

Keep proposed changes in a dated hold register. Apply reason-before-rank: record the scheduling concern and consequence of delay before the proposed label. Add the submitting role and the review appointment after the audit week. Do not copy a proposal into the source record during the freeze.

The audit chair will review held proposals from 2026-08-31, preserving their original submission dates. A proposal that needs clarification returns to its submitting coordinator with a question and a response date. Its age does not establish its merits.

The cabin example is located through DEMO-AIR-0002, ISSUE-0111, and TASK-0297. It shows where a reference belongs in the hold register without proposing a change for that record.

Implementation: PERSON-0010 will brief station clerks on the distinction between initial intake and later changes before the freeze begins. Retain the briefing note with the audit calendar.
