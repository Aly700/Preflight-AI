POLICY
Effective date: 2026-10-07
Custodian: PERSON-0023, TEAM-0005
Scope: Closing paperwork for the fictional issue queue.

1. Write a closure note that identifies the original coordination question, the administrative disposition, the references supporting that disposition, the recorder, and the date. Explain where a later reader can find the underlying paperwork. A bare statement that the matter is finished does not provide a traceable account.

2. Distinguish the issue disposition from task entries. A task entry alone does not establish that an issue should be closed. If the note cites a task, explain its documentary relevance rather than copying its label as the entire explanation.

3. Index attachments before filing the note. Put the attachment heading and filing location on the cover sheet, and retain the sequence in which the papers were received. A missing attachment becomes a request to the records clerk with an owner and a due date.

4. State any continuing administrative obligation and the role that will carry it. If the obligation belongs in another queue, retain the destination reference with the note. Do not hide a future records request inside a concluding sentence.

5. Keep this note about paperwork only. It must contain no physical work directions or judgment about an aircraft. ISSUE-0101, TASK-0268, and TASK-0270 provide locating references for the hydraulics filing example.

Revision note: This edition adds the attachment index and a separate line for continuing administrative obligations.
