RETROSPECTIVE — Weekend Cabin Coverage Needed a Fallback
Date: 2026-10-20
Program: DEMO-PROGRAM-ALPHA
Recorder: PERSON-0025, TEAM-0001

What happened
The cabin weekend plan reserved contact appointments at 10:40 but did not give the relief desk an explicit fallback when a receiving coordinator could not attend. The handover relied on the calendar entry to explain who would take the next call. ISSUE-0103 and TASK-0274 locate the planning folder.

What helped
The dated appointment diary preserved the intended contact and its purpose. The question parking margin also kept background questions beside an addressee and promised reply date. Those details let the program chair distinguish the coverage decision from the separate documentation discussion located through TASK-0275.

What changes
Write a fallback role beside the named receiving coordinator and confirm the covered interval before the preceding weekday handover. If the fallback cannot attend, the duty chair must name the next receiving role and record a contact time. Keep the original appointment visible when the arrangement changes.

The parking margin remains useful, but coverage uncertainty belongs in the main decision paragraph. A side question must not hide who carries the next administrative action. TASK-0276 remains a separate locating reference if attached papers need discussion.

Follow-up
PERSON-0025 will review the fallback line with the weekend roster clerk on 2026-10-23. The program chair will keep the earlier plan with the revised coverage sheet so the reason for the change remains clear.
