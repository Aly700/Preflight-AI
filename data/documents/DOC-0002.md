POLICY
Effective date: 2026-10-04
Custodian: PERSON-0003, TEAM-0003
Scope: Ownership entries on unresolved-issue cover sheets.

1. Every unresolved issue must have an owning team. Write that team in the owning-team box, separate from the individual assignment box. Attendance at a meeting and authorship of a note do not establish ownership.

2. When the person box is blank, the owning team's shift lead remains responsible for arranging the next queue action and naming a suitable person. Leave the field blank until the assignment is actually recorded. Never borrow a name from a neighboring task or from the sheet's author line.

3. Use receiver echo when desk responsibility moves: the receiver repeats the next coordination action and its due date. The sending lead records that response in the transfer margin. Until the receiving arrangement is confirmed, the sending desk continues the follow-up; a proposed transfer must not create an ownership gap.

4. Refer a disagreement about the owning team to the duty chair before shift end. Include the competing scope descriptions and the action that needs an owner. The chair writes the decision beside the original question so the reasons remain available at the following handover.

5. Keep issue ownership distinct from task assignment. ISSUE-0141, TASK-0373, and TASK-0374 are locating references for the controls example; the example does not reassign their records.

Revision note: The separate team and person boxes supersede an ambiguous single responsible-party line.
