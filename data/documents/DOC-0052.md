What happened
During the hydraulics desk's intake cycle, the scheduling request stayed with the collecting papers instead of reaching the review roster clerk. The diary line mixed the expected attachment delivery with the intended reviewer conversation. ISSUE-0029 and TASK-0077 locate the example folder; TASK-0076 and TASK-0078 remain separately referenced discussion papers.

What helped
The desk coordinator compared the collecting list with the appointment diary before handover. That comparison exposed the missing scheduling action without needing a claim about the underlying task categories. The collecting clerk could explain when the papers were expected, and the roster clerk could separately discuss receiving availability.

What changes
Give the diary separate lines for paper delivery and reviewer availability. Each line needs a responsible role, contact time, and the action expected from that contact. The reviewer request must reach the roster clerk even when gathering work continues.

The incoming coordinator will compare the diary against the collecting list at the next handover. A paper-delivery promise must not stand in for a named receiving reviewer. Preserve a moved appointment beside its original entry so the reason for delay remains visible.

Follow-up
