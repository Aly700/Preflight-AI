RETROSPECTIVE — A Structures Attachment Filed Under the Meeting Name
Date: 2026-10-03
Program: DEMO-PROGRAM-BETA
Recorder: PERSON-0022, TEAM-0004

What happened
A structures review attachment was filed under the meeting's short name rather than the heading supplied by the collecting desk. The next clerk searched the source heading and could not locate the paper in the expected folder. ISSUE-0172 and TASK-0458 identify the discussion folder for this retrospective.

What helped
The appointment diary retained the collecting role and the date the paper was expected. The filing clerk used that context to trace the local meeting heading. Preserving the received attachment made it possible to compare the source title with the index label without recreating the paper from memory.

What changes
Record source heading and filing heading together on the index. When a location is corrected, keep a dated note of the original folder route and the receiving location. A quiet refile would solve the immediate search while leaving the next clerk unable to explain the earlier trail.

The review coordinator will attach the location-correction note to the original collecting request. The incoming desk should know which heading to search and which role can explain a mismatch. A meeting title may remain a useful alias, but it must not displace the source heading.

Follow-up
PERSON-0022 will review the dual-heading index with the station records clerk on 2026-10-05. Retain the ambiguous meeting label on the teaching copy to show why the correction was needed.
