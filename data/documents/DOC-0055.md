RETROSPECTIVE — Priority Proposals Changed Faster Than Their Reasons
Date: 2026-10-05
Program: DEMO-PROGRAM-ALPHA
Recorder: PERSON-0004, TEAM-0004

What happened
During a hydraulics agenda discussion, proposed priority labels were recopied while their original reasons remained on separate desk notes. The later agenda made the suggestions look like a settled sequence of decisions. ISSUE-0086 and TASK-0230 locate the paperwork example; this account does not state a record's priority.

What helped
The documentation clerk retained the dated source notes. Comparing the reasons exposed that the proposals addressed different scheduling concerns. The chair could then ask which concern needed a decision instead of treating the newest label as the most persuasive.

What changes
Use reason-before-rank on every proposed change. State the scheduling concern and consequence of delay before the proposed label. Keep the submitting role, date, and reason together in the proposal history, and distinguish that history from the source-record field.

When a proposal changes, preserve the earlier entry and explain what new administrative consideration prompted the revision. Recopying a rank without its reason must not make it appear that a decision has already occurred. The receiving coordinator should be able to find the rationale without asking the outgoing desk to reconstruct the conversation.

Follow-up
PERSON-0004 will review the proposal-history layout with the documentation clerk on 2026-10-07. The chair will check that the next agenda identifies the decision sought and retains the original reasons beside the alternatives.
