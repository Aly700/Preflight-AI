Date: 2026-10-21
To: DEMO-PROGRAM-ALPHA queue coordinators
From: PERSON-0011, TEAM-0005
Subject: Glossary Practice for ALPHA Queue Coordinators

Decision: DEMO-PROGRAM-ALPHA will hold a glossary practice session on 2026-10-22 at 12:40 for intake clerks and queue coordinators. The session concentrates on record vocabulary that can be confused during a spoken handover.

The trainer will distinguish issue status from task status, task kind from subsystem, and exact open from the collective term unresolved. Use the existing desk glossaries as the vocabulary source. Do not invent shorter category names merely because they are easier to say during a call.

A dictation exercise will have the receiving clerk write labels with their underscores intact and name the record type beside an ambiguous term. A separate blank-field exercise will distinguish an absent person assignment from an absent owning-team entry. The recorder's name must not be used to fill either example by inference.

Use ISSUE-0023 and TASK-0060 as locating references for the electrical training folder. TASK-0061 and TASK-0062 may identify separate review discussions on the exercise sheet. The session does not supply values for those records.

Implementation: PERSON-0011 will prepare the blank exercise sheet before the session. The trainer will collect unclear definitions as questions for the records coordinator, retain the original spelling, and attach the answer to the teaching copy. The station clerk will keep that copy beside the intake reference binder.
