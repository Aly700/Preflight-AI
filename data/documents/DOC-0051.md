RETROSPECTIVE — A Reopen Request Without Its Return Reason
Date: 2026-09-18
Program: DEMO-PROGRAM-ALPHA
Recorder: PERSON-0012, TEAM-0006

What happened
A cabin desk request asked for an earlier discussion to be brought back but omitted why it needed another hearing. The receiving clerk could find the old question, yet could not tell what administrative point the requester wanted reconsidered. ISSUE-0048 and TASK-0128 locate the folder used in this paperwork retrospective.

What helped
The records clerk preserved the earlier question rather than editing it to match the new request. The reviewer could therefore ask the requesting coordinator for a distinct explanation. Keeping the request separate also prevented the desk conversation from being treated as evidence of a record transition.

What changes
Apply the return-note rule to the request: retain the earlier question and place a separate dated return reason beneath it. The coordinator must explain the new administrative question and why the earlier note does not answer it. Add the named reviewer and intended discussion date before handing the request to the receiving desk.

If the explanation is missing, the collecting clerk returns a precise question to the requester and records the next contact time. The clerk must not invent the reason from a short subject heading.

Follow-up
PERSON-0012 will review the revised request layout on 2026-09-21. The training copy will show distinct spaces for the earlier question, new reason, reviewer name, and next discussion appointment.
