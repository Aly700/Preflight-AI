MEMORANDUM
Date: 2026-10-24
To: DEMO-PROGRAM-BETA queue coordinators
From: PERSON-0003, TEAM-0003
Subject: Retiring the Single-Line Queue Cover Form

Decision: From 2026-10-26, DEMO-PROGRAM-BETA will stop using the single-line queue cover form for new paperwork. Its combined responsible-party line made it difficult to distinguish an owning team, a receiving reviewer, and the role arranging the next contact.

The replacement layout provides separate spaces for those roles, the locating reference, the question requiring a decision, and the next contact time. Existing papers retain their original form. Add a dated cross-reference sheet when a later discussion needs the new layout, preserving the earlier cover rather than silently recopying its contents.

The station records clerk will move unused old forms to a clearly labelled reference folder. Keep a blank specimen for interpreting earlier paperwork, but remove it from the tray used for new requests. This preserves filing history without encouraging the old layout to circulate again.

Use DEMO-AIR-0008 and ISSUE-0031 to locate the hydraulics example. TASK-0082, TASK-0083, and TASK-0084 may identify separate attachment references; the form change does not combine their responsibilities.

Implementation: PERSON-0003 will give the new blank layout to the station clerk on 2026-10-25. The desk coordinator will check the first handover using it for role clarity and record any ambiguous label beside the specimen retained for teaching.
