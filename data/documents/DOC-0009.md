Desk Vocabulary for Issues and Priority Requests
Compiled: 2026-06-08
Compiler: Demo Worker 0036, Demo Team 06
Use: Wording aid for fictional queue correspondence.

ISSUE TERMS

open — An issue status value. Use it for that exact value, not as a loose replacement for every issue still requiring queue attention.

in_progress — An issue status value indicating progress within the tracking workflow. Keep the underscore when writing a field value. The same spelling also occurs in the task vocabulary, so name the record type.

blocked — An issue status value for the blocked category. Under the record contract, an issue in this category has an unfinished associated task. A paper routing delay alone does not establish the category.

reopened — An issue status value supported by a closure event followed by a reopening event. Its current closure timestamp is empty. A renewed conversation does not itself establish that history.

closed — An issue status value with a populated closure timestamp. It describes the issue record, not the completion of every conversation that may mention it.

unresolved — A collective query term for issue statuses other than closed. It includes open, in_progress, blocked, and reopened. Writers must say whether they mean this collective term or the exact open category.

PRIORITY WORDS

low, medium, high — The exact permitted priority labels, in increasing order of queue priority. They are separate from status. In a request, put a proposed value under proposal and a reason under justification. Never infer priority from the darkness of handwriting or an envelope's position.

TASK WORDS

todo, in_progress, awaiting_review, completed, blocked — Task statuses. The awaiting_review category has an empty completion timestamp; completed has a populated one. Task completion does not automatically close an issue.

review, investigation, documentation, inspection, rectification — Task kind labels used to organize tracking entries. Here they identify categories only. They provide no directions for physical activity.

SUBSYSTEM WORDS

hydraulics, avionics, electrical, cabin, structures, controls — Exact grouping labels on issue records. Copy their spelling when identifying a filter; do not turn a subsystem label into a team name.

REFERENCE USE

ISSUE-0101, DEMO-AIR-0007, and TASK-0269 may serve as locating references in a correspondence question. Their appearance on this vocabulary sheet supplies no record values. Ask the records for such values when a discussion needs them.

Editorial note: Keep the record type beside ambiguous terms. This sheet defines vocabulary, not a snapshot of the queue.
