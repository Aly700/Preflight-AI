POLICY
Effective date: 2026-10-26
Custodian: PERSON-0014, TEAM-0002
Scope: Recurring backlog review for DEMO-PROGRAM-ALPHA.

1. Hold the Friday queue sweep at 09:10 and reserve twenty minutes for backlog coordination. This edition formalizes the existing desk appointment. Circulate topic headings before the meeting so that owning-team leads can prepare useful decisions.

2. Review aging questions, missing ownership entries, absent reviewer coverage, and administrative dependencies. Ask what action would move each discussion forward. A backlog meeting should produce an owner and a date, not simply repeat the preceding week's subject headings.

3. Keep priority proposals distinct from scheduling decisions. The chair may arrange a discussion slot without changing a priority field. A proposed rank change requires a written reason through the separate request process.

4. Maintain a dated carry list containing the locating reference, owning team, next action, and next review date. Put parked background questions beside their addressee and promised response date. A parked question must remain visible when the main agenda advances.

5. End by reading the carry list to the receiving desk roles. Retain the prior list alongside the new sheet so that deferred decisions remain traceable. ISSUE-0014, TASK-0036, and TASK-0037 are references for the hydraulics filing example, not a statement about backlog membership.

Revision note: This edition records the standing appointment and standardizes the owner, action, and next-review columns.
