MEETING NOTES — Who Carries a Cabin Item With No Person Assignment
Date: 2026-08-27
Program: DEMO-PROGRAM-BETA

Attendees: PERSON-0034 for TEAM-0004; partner team's shift lead; documentation coordinator; duty chair.

Purpose: Agree responsibility when a queue discussion encounters a blank person assignment field. The cabin folder is located through ISSUE-0032 and TASK-0087.

Decisions
The owning team remains responsible for an unresolved issue even when no person assignment is recorded. Its shift lead arranges the next administrative contact and selects a suitable person through the assignment process. The meeting recorder's name must not fill the blank by default.

The documentation coordinator may gather a paper without becoming the task assignee. Put the collecting role and promised delivery date on the attachment request, separate from the ownership fields. This distinction lets the next shift continue paper collection without assuming an assignment decision occurred.

A dispute about which team owns the discussion goes to the duty chair before handover. Preserve the competing scope descriptions and write the chair's decision beside them. The sending desk carries the next contact until a receiving arrangement is confirmed.
