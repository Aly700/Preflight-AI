POLICY
Effective date: 2026-09-12
Custodian: PERSON-0010, TEAM-0004
Scope: Administrative requests to revisit issue closure paperwork.

1. A reopen needs a reason and a named reviewer. Explain what new question requires attention and why the earlier closure note does not answer it. A label such as revisit is insufficient. The reviewer must be a named person on the request, not only a department or desk.

2. Apply the return-note rule: preserve the earlier question and write the reason for returning beneath it as a separate dated entry. Do not rewrite the earlier account to make the later concern appear to have been present throughout.

3. The originating shift lead obtains the reviewer's acceptance within that shift. If coverage is unavailable, the duty chair arranges a substitute and records the revised discussion time. The missing reviewer belongs on the handover list until that arrangement is made.

4. Attach the earlier closure-note reference and identify which administrative statement is being reconsidered. The review concerns completeness and traceability of paperwork. Physical instructions and technical conclusions do not belong on this request.

5. After discussion, the recorder preserves the reason, reviewer name, meeting date, and resulting paperwork action together. Use ISSUE-0177 and TASK-0471 only to locate the avionics example when explaining the layout; this policy does not report a reopening.

Revision note: The dated return reason and explicit reviewer acceptance are now required parts of the request.
