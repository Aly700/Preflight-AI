Subject: Name the Reviewer Before the Shift Ends

Decision: When a task is presented for coordination in the awaiting_review category, the owning team's shift lead must name a reviewer within the same shift. This DEMO-PROGRAM-ALPHA rule concerns reviewer scheduling and the completeness of the appointment record.

The receiving reviewer must confirm availability. Put the person's name, expected discussion time, and substitute contact on the appointment sheet. A team label alone is not a reviewer name, and an open calendar interval is not acceptance of the appointment.

If the intended reviewer is unavailable, the shift lead asks the duty chair for coverage before handover. Retain the initial request and the reason for changing the appointment. The incoming desk needs a concrete receiving role and next contact time, not an instruction to find somebody later.

The cabin example uses DEMO-AIR-0007, ISSUE-0089, and TASK-0239 as locating references. Keep gathering requests associated with TASK-0237 separate from the appointment sheet, and retain TASK-0238 only in its own reference space when needed.

Implementation: PERSON-0019 will brief the roster clerks on 2026-09-05. The program chair will review the appointment layout on 2026-09-11, focusing on whether a shift lead can identify the receiving reviewer and fallback without consulting an earlier shift's private notes.
