POLICY
Effective date: 2026-08-11
Custodian: PERSON-0025, TEAM-0001
Scope: New issue intake and queue triage.

1. Use two-touch intake. The intake clerk first records the sender's question and supplied reference. The receiving coordinator then checks the destination and expected reply date. Include the subsystem label and the team proposed to own the issue so that intake ends with a usable coordination entry.

2. Set priority at intake with a written reason. Follow reason-before-rank: describe the scheduling concern and consequence of delay before selecting low, medium, or high. Put the reason above the priority box. A copied subject heading is not a reason, and a crowded desk is not evidence about an individual issue.

3. Record an owning team before passing the entry to the queue clerk. If a person has not been selected, ask that team's shift lead to arrange the person assignment; do not substitute the intake clerk merely because that clerk prepared the sheet.

4. Put intake disagreements on an amber query card. The triage chair assigns the question an addressee and a response date before the intake session ends. Keep the original submission attached so the next reader can distinguish the sender's account from the chair's decision.

5. Use ISSUE-0084 and DEMO-AIR-0009 as locating references when teaching the electrical intake layout. Their inclusion supplies no priority assessment.

Revision note: This edition adds the reason-above-priority layout and the amber query card to the intake checklist.
