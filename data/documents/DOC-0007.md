POLICY
Effective date: 2026-07-06
Custodian: PERSON-0034, TEAM-0004
Scope: Successive shift transfer of queue coordination work.

1. Prepare a carry list before the outgoing desk closes its shift. Give each entry a locating reference, owning team, next administrative action, due date, and receiving role. Keep coverage exceptions beside the action they affect instead of placing them in an unlabelled end note.

2. Add a context strip to each packet. It carries the question being handed over, the reply destination, and the next contact time. The strip must remain useful if the packet becomes separated from the shift sheet.

3. The incoming clerk compares the carry list with the desk index at the handover conversation. Resolve an omitted reference while the outgoing coordinator is still available. If the paper cannot be located, record its expected filing location and who will search the index.

4. State who covers each subsystem desk during the incoming shift. A desk coverage arrangement does not change a task's person assignment. If the usual coordinator is absent, record the substitute role and the time at which normal coverage is expected to resume.

5. Preserve the outgoing list as a dated sheet. For the cabin example, ISSUE-0020 and TASK-0054 may locate the relevant paperwork without establishing its present queue position.

Revision note: The incoming comparison with the desk index is added to prevent silent omissions between shifts.
