MEETING NOTES — Cabin Attachment Filing Across the Records Desks
Date: 2026-07-07
Program: DEMO-PROGRAM-ALPHA

Attendees: PERSON-0021 for TEAM-0003; partner team's records liaison; station filing clerk; cabin desk coordinator.

Purpose: Agree how cabin attachments will be indexed across the records desks at DEMO-STATION-02. ISSUE-0073, TASK-0192, and TASK-0193 locate the discussion material.

Decisions
The shared filing list will retain the source heading beside the local filing heading. A document may arrive with a sender's title that differs from the station's index label; keeping both lets a later clerk retrace the search without guessing which subject was intended.

If a paper is found under a different folder heading, keep a dated location-correction note with the source route and the receiving location. Do not silently refile it and discard the trail. The collecting coordinator needs to know where a search began as well as where the paper was found.

The partner liaison will confirm which attachment heading belongs to each discussion reference. TEAM-0003 will supply the receiving filing contact, while the cabin desk coordinator retains the appointment with the requester.
