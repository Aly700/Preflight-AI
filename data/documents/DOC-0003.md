POLICY
Effective date: 2026-10-14
Custodian: PERSON-0018, TEAM-0006
Scope: Coordination follow-up for items selected from the blocked-issue queue.

1. The owning-team lead reviews the dependency slip at 10:15 and again at 15:15 each working day. Record the answer needed, the team being contacted, and the next promised contact time. These checkpoints organize follow-up; they do not describe the condition of an aircraft.

2. At the morning checkpoint, ask whether the dependency has a named contact and a useful next action. At the afternoon checkpoint, carry any unanswered request into the next shift's list with the same owning team. Preserve a missed contact as a dated entry instead of erasing it when a new appointment is made.

3. Use question-first escalation if the contact cannot be arranged. Begin with the answer needed, then identify the role responsible for seeking it and the next contact time. Send that request to the duty chair. A general instruction to hurry gives the chair no decision to make.

4. Keep scheduling escalation separate from a priority-change request. A delayed answer requires a follow-up plan; any proposed priority change needs its own written justification and review.

5. For the controls desk example, file the dependency slip under DEMO-AIR-0001 and ISSUE-0077. TASK-0203 may identify the associated discussion without implying any recorded category.

Revision note: This edition introduces the afternoon carry-forward check and preserves missed-contact history.
