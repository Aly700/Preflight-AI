RETROSPECTIVE — Duplicate Intake Tickets Lost Their Shared Source
Date: 2026-09-01
Program: DEMO-PROGRAM-ALPHA
Recorder: PERSON-0026, TEAM-0002

What happened
During an electrical intake discussion, a copied card was treated as a fresh submission because its source heading had been shortened. The desk began a separate collecting conversation before comparing the original question. ISSUE-0074 and TASK-0195 locate the teaching folder; TASK-0194 remains a separate attachment reference.

What helped
The filing clerk retained the sender's original heading on the index copy. Comparing that heading with the intake card made the relationship between the papers understandable without relying on recollection. The desk could then discuss whether the scope was genuinely shared instead of assuming that similar subsystem labels proved a duplicate.

What changes
The intake clerk will check the origin heading before preparing a new cover. Use two-touch intake to preserve the sender's question at the first touch and verify the destination and expected reply date at the receiving touch. That conversation should also identify whether the submission refers back to an existing question.

Any merge proposal keeps both issue references on the eventual cover sheet, with a reason for choosing the continuing entry. This retrospective does not identify a duplicate partner for the teaching reference.

Follow-up
PERSON-0026 will review the revised intake-card layout with the filing clerk on 2026-09-03. The origin-heading space and the sender's question must remain readable after copying.
